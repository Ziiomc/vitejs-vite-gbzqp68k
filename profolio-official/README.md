# ProFolio demo

This is an English-first interactive sales demo with an optional Spanish interface.

- The homes, prices, photos, and summary metrics are illustrative. No listing is verified or connected to live inventory.
- Area and budget filters, favorites, Open Tour planning, a demo profile, and listing drafts run in the browser. Their data is saved only in local storage on that device.
- Open Tour distance and time are rough straight-line estimates. Manual stops are not placed on the map until geocoding is connected.
- Video tours, Off-Market inventory, live AI, accounts, online publishing, payments, and subscriptions are planned features, not live services.

For a commercial launch, connect verified listing data, server-side accounts and storage, a supported payment flow, and operational review of each published claim.
